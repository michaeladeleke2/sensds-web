# Levels and badges

As on the desktop, students earn XP and badges in the Test tab:

- Try a Gesture prediction: +10 XP. RoboSoccer gesture that fires: +15 XP. Maze solved: +25 XP,
  plus 15 for three stars or 5 for two.
- Levels: Beginner (0), Explorer (100), Intermediate (300), Advanced (600), Expert (1000).
- Badges: First Look, High Five (5 predictions), Sharp Eye (80%+ confidence), On Fire (20),
  Soccer Rookie, Soccer Pro (10 gestures), Maze Rookie, Maze Explorer (3), Maze Master (5),
  Perfect Run (3 stars), Speed Runner (10 moves or fewer).

The level chip in the top bar shows the level and XP. Click it to see every badge. A new badge slides
in at the top right. Like the desktop, progress lasts until the page is closed.
